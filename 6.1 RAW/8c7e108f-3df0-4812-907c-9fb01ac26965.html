A monomial is an algebraic expression with exactly one term: 5x2 A binomial is an algebraic expression with exactly two terms: 5x2+6y3 A trinomial is an algebraic expression with exactly three terms: 5x2+6y3−7c Monomials, binomials, and trinomials are all different types of polynomials. A polynomial is a monomial or two or more monomials combined by addition or subtraction. The degree of a polynomial is the highest degree of all its terms.
